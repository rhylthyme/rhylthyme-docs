⏳ Brew along

The night runs at 30× (change Spell speed in the player). Press Begin the brewing. Some steps wait for you: press Now when Professor Hoot reaches the window, and That's done when the frogs are caught or the elixir stops giggling. The tower and the schedule follow along.

    📜 The spell log

      Everything on this page runs in your browser from three script tags: the engine (src/index.js), the player (src/element.js) and the validator (browser/schedule.js). The recipe book uses validateProgram and findingCodes; the schedule is itinerary(program, { finishAt }); the tower and the log listen only to rt-step-began and rt-step-ended, and know nothing about potions: a station lights up for whatever step holds its resource.
